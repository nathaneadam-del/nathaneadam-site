# Testimonial shortlist — from 13 years of course evaluations

Pulled 1 September 2026 from *Nathan Adam Course Evaluations 2013-2026.pdf*
(360 pages, roughly 4,000 comments, Fall 2013 to Fall 2025). Every quote below
is verbatim from the PDF except where marked `[…]` for a trim. Typos are left
as the student wrote them; say if you want them cleaned.

The evaluations are anonymous, so each one needs a name from you before it
goes on the site. Term and course are given so you can place them. One
student signed his: Steven Kim, Spring 2015.

**One attribution caution.** The PDF includes a Fall 2016 report with no term
on its title page (creation date 23 Dec 2016), and several of its comments
appear again inside the Fall 2017 report. Quotes marked *Fall 2016* below come
from that report; if your memory says 2017, trust your memory.

Picked for specificity, and for what the AI audience needs to hear: that
somebody who was intimidated by a piece of software learned it, fast, from
you. Grouped by what each one proves.
