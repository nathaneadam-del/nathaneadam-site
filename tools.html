Tools

Small things I’ve built for people whose craft is changing under them. They run entirely in your browser, which means your answers stay on your own machine and never reach me. Nothing here is a lead magnet with a course bolted to the back of it.

Diagnostic · about 2 minutes

Which half of your work is actually at risk?

Ten questions about how you actually get paid, and then my honest read on which part of what you do is exposed and which part isn’t. No score, no personality type. Both of those would be numbers I made up.

More coming

There’s a second one in progress for people who teach: you tell it what your assignment currently asks students to hand in, and it gives you back a version built around process and defence instead of the final artefact. That one comes out of a paper I presented at MEIEA this year.

If there’s something you keep doing by hand that a small tool would fix, tell me. nathan@nathaneadam.com. I can’t promise to build it, but I’ve built stranger things for worse reasons. :)
